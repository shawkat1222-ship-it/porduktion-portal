# Produktion Portal

**NORDFROST GmbH** — Production Shift Report Portal

A structured multi-step form for shift supervisors to document production data at the end of each shift. Covers:

1. **Störungsprotokoll** — Incidents, downtimes, and corrective actions
2. **Palettenmenge & Mitarbeiter** — Pallet output and staffing levels
3. **Restmengenzählung** — Remaining materials count at shift end
4. **Palettenstatus** — NOK (not-OK) pallets with categorization
5. **Speichern & Senden** — Validate, export as JSON, or prepare email draft

Audience: Production team leads and shift supervisors at NORDFROST.
